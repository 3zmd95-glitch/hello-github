## Social Analytics page spec (the page to replicate)

URL: /post-activity/home/post-activity. One page, four stacked zones, driven by a platform filter (All / Instagram / YouTube / TikTok / Threads). All numbers are averages over recent posts (Beacons does not state the window; the media kit says "last 30 days").

1. Header: title "Social Analytics", platform dropdown, "Ask Beam" button.
2. Overview (All view): four KPI cards Total Followers, Avg. Engagement, Avg. Likes, Avg. Views, plus an AI button "What changed this week?".
3. Platform cards (All view): one card per connected account with platform icon, handle, external link, and rows Followers (Subscribers for YouTube), Engagement, Avg. Likes, Avg. Views.
4. Post Activity: three counters Posts last 90 days, Posts last month, Posts last week, plus AI button "What platform drives the most engagement?" (per platform: "How often should I post on X?").

| Threads | Total Followers, Engagement, Avg. Likes, Avg. Views, Avg. Shares, Avg. Comments | none |

Metric definitions to implement (Beacons hides its formulas, so the rebuilt numbers will not match to the decimal):

- Avg. likes / views / comments / shares = mean over posts published in the window (use 30 days, fall back to last 20 posts when fewer than 5 posts).
- Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ views × 100 per post, averaged; show the by-followers variant ((likes + comments + shares) ÷ followers × 100) as a secondary figure, since brands ask for it.
- Total Followers (All) = sum of followers across platforms (1.5k = 272 + 6 + 1.2k + 0).
- Avg. Engagement / Likes / Views (All) = mean of the platform values that have data.
- Posts last 90 days / month / week = count of posts by publish timestamp, per platform and summed.
- "What changed this week?" = compare this week's snapshot with last week's and describe deltas (an LLM call over the diff, or a plain rule-based sentence).

Visual style: white cards on a light grey page, 8-12 px radius, Poppins font, platform icons on cards, thin horizontal bars for demographics, black / grey pie for gender, small ↗ external-link icons, AI buttons outlined in blue with a sparkle icon. The Arabic version should mirror the layout (RTL) and keep numbers in Latin digits.

## Build plan

Build the data layer first and the Social Analytics page second; everything else Beacons does stays on Beacons until he asks to move it. Four phases, each shippable on its own.

1. Phase 0, setup (with Abdulaziz present, about one hour): confirm the target site and stack; create the Meta app (Instagram + Threads products, add his accounts as testers), the Google Cloud project (YouTube Data + Analytics APIs, OAuth client), and the TikTok developer app (sandbox first). Run each OAuth flow once and store the tokens in a server-side secret store (.env on his machine or the host's secrets), never in front-end code. Reconnect Instagram in Beacons too, so the acceptance numbers become comparable.
2. Phase 1, data layer: one scheduled job (GitHub Actions cron, a small Node / Python script on the host, or n8n) that runs daily, pulls the endpoints above, computes the metrics in the spec, and writes snapshots. Store in SQLite or Supabase with three tables: platform\_daily (platform, date, followers, avg\_views, avg\_likes, avg\_comments, avg\_shares, engagement\_rate, posts\_7d, posts\_30d, posts\_90d), posts (platform, post\_id, published\_at, kind \[video, short, reel, story, thread\], views, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch\_time\_s, permalink, thumb\_url) and demographics (platform, snapshot\_date, dimension \[gender, age, country, city\], key, pct). Keep every daily row; week-over-week deltas come from it.
3. Phase 2, dashboard UI: one route that renders the Social Analytics spec from the snapshots: platform filter, KPI row, platform cards, per-platform overview and demographics (pie + horizontal bars), post-activity counters, plus a My Content grid (top posts this week, per-platform post search, CSV export). Arabic (Hijazi) first with RTL layout and an English toggle. Add "What changed this week?" as a text block generated from the last two weekly snapshots.
4. Phase 3, optional extras that Beacons also provides: link-click analytics if the link page moves off Beacons (needs his own redirect endpoint logging source, device, country), email capture into his own list, and a pricing-calculator page reusing the follower and view numbers.

What stays on Beacons for now and why: Smart Reply (needs Instagram messaging permissions and Meta App Review), the store and payouts, email broadcasts (500 sends/month included in his plan), and the media kit (its numbers can later be fed from his own snapshots, but the hosted page and email-lock are Beacons features).

Acceptance test for Phase 2: with Instagram reconnected, the rebuilt page shows the same follower counts as Beacons (TikTok 1.2k, Instagram 272, YouTube 6, Threads 0), TikTok engagement within about 1 point of 7.8%, and the same demographic ordering (Saudi Arabia first on both platforms, 25-34 the largest age bucket).
