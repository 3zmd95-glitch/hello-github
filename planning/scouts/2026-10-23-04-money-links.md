## 2. What the reference tools do (public docs)
- **Beacons** (owner's account, see `references/beacons/notes.md`): 4 link pages, blocks, per-link clicks, Sources/Devices/Location, media kit with synced stats + "Work with me", Audience CRM (email, phone, name, source, platform, engagement, added on), free LUT product (51 "sales"), welcome automation. Audience CSV: grey download icon in Audience ([help](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4703745); CSV import needs only an `email` first column, [help](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4703361)). A domain obtained through Beacons "will always remain the property of Beacons AI Inc.", no DNS access, **$70 one-time transfer on request**, cancelling returns the account to Free and unlinks the domain ([ownership](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4696129), [cancel](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4695169)). Pricing calculator: accounts x deliverables + add-ons ([page](https://home.beacons.ai/app-pages/pricing-calculator)).
- **Cloudflare free limits**: Durable Objects SQLite on Free = 100k requests, 100k rows written, 5M rows read a day, 5 GB ([DO pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/)); Workers Free = 100k requests/day, 10 ms CPU, 50 subrequests ([pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), [limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/)); static assets free and unlimited, 25 MiB/file ([billing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/billing-and-limitations/)); a Worker custom domain (apex ok) needs the domain to be an active Cloudflare zone ([docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/routing/custom-domains/)), and the Free plan supports the full (nameserver) setup ([DNS](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/zone-setups/full-setup/)); Turnstile free ([plans](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/plans/)); R2 10 GB, free egress ([pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/)).
- **Email**: Resend Free = 3,000/month, 100/day, 1,000 marketing contacts, 3 domains ([pricing](https://resend.com/pricing)); `POST /emails` takes `headers`, a scheduled send time, an `Idempotency-Key` ([API](https://resend.com/docs/api-reference/emails/send-email)); send from a subdomain such as `mail.3zprod.com` ([domains](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/domains/introduction)). Cloudflare Email Sending to arbitrary recipients is **beta and needs Workers Paid** ([docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/)); Email Routing (receive, forward `hello@3zprod.com` to Gmail) is free. Decision: Resend sends, Routing receives.
- **Moyasar**: fees are "provided by our sales team" ([FAQ](https://moyasar.com/en/solutions/startups-and-freelancers/)); a third-party blog quotes mada 1% + SAR 1, cards 2.2% + SAR 1, no monthly fee ([Creole Studios](https://www.creolestudios.com/ksa/?p=7847), unverified) vs Beacons' 9%. `POST /invoices` returns a hosted payment `url` with `callback_url`/`success_url` ([API](https://docs.moyasar.com/api/invoices/01-create-invoice)); account webhooks (`payment_paid`) are HTTPS POSTs whose body carries `secret_token`, retried 5 times at 1/10/30/60/120 min ([ref](https://docs.moyasar.com/api/other/webhooks/webhook-reference)). So the Worker is the whole server.
- **PDF**: browsers shape Arabic natively; JS PDF libraries (jsPDF, pdfmake) do not join/reorder Arabic without add-ons such as bidi-shaper. Print CSS = zero cost. Cloudflare Browser Rendering `pdf` (Worker binding `env.BROWSER.quickAction`, [docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/browser-rendering/rest-api/pdf-endpoint/)) gives a real PDF; Free = 10 browser-minutes/day ([limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/browser-rendering/platform/limits/)).
- **Hosting alternatives**: GitHub Pages 2nd repo needs apex A records 185.199.108-111.153 ([docs](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/configuring-a-custom-domain-for-your-github-pages-site/managing-a-custom-domain-for-your-github-pages-site)) but is static: no click logging, and `/go` would still need Cloudflare DNS. Rejected.

## 5. Risks
1. **Domain ownership**: public DNS shows 3zprod.com on name.com nameservers, A record 34.49.161.242 (a Google Cloud IP, Beacons' host), registered 2026-06-01, expires 2027-06-01 (RDAP). The brief said Hostinger; nothing points there. If bought through Beacons: no DNS access and cancelling unlinks it. Fix before anything else. If the owner has his own name.com login: change nameservers there (1-48 h, same idea as [Hostinger's guide](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1696789-how-to-change-nameservers-at-hostinger/)) after copying records. There is no MX record at all (no mailbox on 3zprod.com today). **Scout `06-website.md` assumes "Hostinger DNS + mailbox"; public DNS contradicts it, so its cut-over section 5 must be re-based on the answer to decision 1.** Both scouts also propose a `SiteStore` Durable Object with migration `v2-site`: build one (this file's `hits`/`contacts`/`leads`/`orders` tables are a superset of 06's W3/W4).
2. **Saudi law (not legal advice)**: PDPL summaries say prior consent + clear sender + easy opt-out for marketing ([Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2023/12/Saudi-Arabias-data-protection-law-enters-into-force), [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/saudi-arabia-publishes-final-personal-data-protection-law)); double opt-in is not stated as mandatory, we use it as proof. Click stats store no IP and no cookie. Data sits at Cloudflare/Resend outside KSA (same as Beacons): say so in the privacy page. **Mawthooq**: GCAM licence for creators earning from ads (news: SAR 15,000 / 3 years, penalties up to SAR 1-5 million, [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2176661/media), [Clyde](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2022/09/new-social-media-requirements-for-saudi-arabia)); a public brand-deal page and rate card make this visible: check GCAM before the first paid deal.
3. **Resend free caps**: 100 mails/day shared by confirmations, welcomes and lead alerts; bots can burn it, hence the caps in #5. Bounce/complaint spikes risk suspension, hence suppression + confirm-first. Apple mail inflates opens: show clicks, not opens.
4. **Affiliate rules**: Amazon bans obscuring the referring site, shorteners that hide Amazon, and (unconfirmed) Special Links in email ([Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/link-cloaking-amazon/)): keep `/go` referrer visible, never put affiliate links in emails. AliExpress not checked.
5. Free-tier ceilings: 10 ms CPU per request (templates only, no heavy libs), 100k Worker requests/day shared with the API, 5 cron triggers (1 used). Viral day: DO write errors are swallowed, links keep working, counts undercount.
6. Money: Moyasar needs approval (master plan says CR/freelance document "in progress"); VAT/ZATCA invoices not researched. KV files 25 MiB; paid files need R2 (card on file unconfirmed).
