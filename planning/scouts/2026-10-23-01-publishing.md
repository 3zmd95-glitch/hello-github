## 2. What the references do (public docs)

- **Metricool** Best times: per network, from the account's own platform data when the API gives it (Instagram via Meta, TikTok **Business** ≥ 100 followers), else generic "behavioral studies" — so for @3z.prod (TikTok Personal, YouTube) Metricool's heat map is generic; ours from real posts would be better. Heat map = days × hours, darker = better, % view ([help](https://help.metricool.com/en/article/best-time-to-post-on-social-media-in-metricool-hj3rgj/)). **Autolists** = a list of posts on chosen days/times, with a "repeat" switch that sends published items to the back (circular evergreen) ([page](https://metricool.com/autolists-social-media/)). **Bulk CSV**: template with one TRUE/FALSE column per network, UTF-8, public media links, ≤ 50 posts per file ([help](https://help.metricool.com/en/article/how-to-schedule-posts-in-batch-with-a-csv-file-in-metricool-3wihqx/)).
- **Instagram** ([publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/content-publishing), [media ref](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media)): media fetched from a **public URL**; carousel = child containers (`is_carousel_item=true`) + `CAROUSEL` with `children`, ≤ 20 items, counts as 1 of 100 posts/24 h; **images JPEG only, ≤ 8 MB**; Reels ≤ 300 MB, 3 s–15 min; **STORIES** image ≤ 8 MB or video 3–60 s ≤ 100 MB, no link/poll stickers, expire in 24 h. Poll `status_code` ≤ 1/min. Top-level comment = `POST /{media-id}/comments?message=` ([ref](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-media/comments)), scope `instagram_business_manage_comments`.
- **Threads** ([posts](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/posts), [replies](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/retrieve-and-manage-replies/create-replies)): carousel 2–20 items, 1 post of 250/24 h; wait ~30 s before publishing a container; reply = `POST /me/threads` with `reply_to_id` then `threads_publish`, scope **`threads_manage_replies`**, 1,000 replies/24 h.
- **TikTok** ([guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines), [media transfer](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide), [photo](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-photo-post)): unaudited = `SELF_ONLY` and only private accounts, 5 users/24 h. **The audit's "Intended Use" excludes "a utility tool to help upload contents to the account(s) you or your team manages"** — our tool is exactly that. PULL_FROM_URL needs a verified domain or URL prefix (a file you upload under the prefix), https, **no redirects**; photos ≤ 35, images ≤ 20 MB; token 6 req/min, **≤ 5 pending shares per 24 h**.
- **YouTube** ([videos.insert](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert), [revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history), [quota](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost)): upload now costs **1 unit in its own bucket, 100 uploads/day** (Dec 4 2025 cut 1,600 → ~100; Jun 1 2026 own bucket) — the brief's "1,600 of 10,000" is outdated; `comments.insert` 50 units from the 10,000 pool (scope `youtube.force-ssl`); no API to pin a comment. **Both Google pages (updated 2026-10-15) now say "Videos uploaded from unverified API projects are not restricted to private viewing mode"** — our `07` doc and the 2020 notice say the opposite.
- **Hosting:** R2 free = 10 GB, 1 M writes, 10 M reads/month, free egress; presigned PUT works on the S3 domain only (CORS needed), r2.dev URLs are "development only" ([pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/), [presigned](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/api/s3/presigned-urls/)). Supabase Free = 1 GB, 5 GB egress, **50 MB per file**, pauses after 1 idle week, no custom domain ([pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing)). B2 = 10 GB free, egress free to 3× stored and free through partner CDNs ([pricing](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/pricing)). Workers Free: request body 100 MB, 10 ms CPU, 50 subrequests ([limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/)). The Worker **can** issue uploads: R2 binding multipart through its own routes (no keys), or an `aws4fetch` presigned PUT (R2/B2).

**A5 model (PR 1):** per platform, last 180 days, drop stories and posts < 72 h old (counters not settled), score = `min(views / platform median, 4)`; bucket Riyadh weekday × 2-hour block; cell = `(Σscore + 2·1)/(n + 2)` (shrink toward "average" so one lucky post cannot win); show **% of best cell** like Metricool; top 3 slots need n ≥ 2; **fewer than 20 posts on a platform → keep static `BEST_TIME` and say "needs 20 posts, you have N"**. Label it "when your posts did best", not "when your audience is online". UI: 3 tappable time chips in the post dialog + a 7-day heat strip in Growth (an hour grid is too wide for a phone).

## 5. Risks

- **TikTok:** ≤ 5 pending inbox shares per 24 h and ~15 posts/day — bulk and queue must cap TikTok at 5/day or the 6th fails. A reviewer asking "who is the audience?" cannot be answered truthfully for a private tool.
- **Bans / spam:** official APIs on the owner's own accounts is fine. Repeated identical captions or first comments, and re-posting the same video, can read as spam/unoriginal on IG and TikTok: vary the text, keep gaps, cap rates. Worker never likes/follows.
- **Google Testing mode:** refresh tokens expire after 7 days ([OAuth docs](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2)) → a YouTube post scheduled > 7 days ahead can fail `token_expired`. `07` claims "YouTube refreshes by itself": only true in Production.
- **Media host:** TikTok may reject a `workers.dev` prefix (`url_ownership_unverified`) → photos have no fallback (videos can use legacy FILE_UPLOAD); custom domain (3zprod.com on Cloudflare) is the cure. R2 needs a billing alert; evergreen media must not be auto-deleted.
- **Budgets:** `publish:jobs` is one KV doc; replies already spend up to 600 of 1,000 daily writes (`51-global-inbox`) → bulk must batch. A 10-slide carousel is ~15 calls of the 34-call run budget → multi-tick state.
- **Meta:** JPEG only (iPhone HEIC must be re-encoded), Reels ≤ 300 MB (iPhone 4K clips exceed Supabase's 50 MB), stories drop link stickers, `threads_manage_replies` and `instagram_business_manage_comments` must be added to the apps + a reconnect.
- **First-comment timing:** Meta documents no wait for Instagram; Threads asks ~30 s before publishing a container. We run it on the next tick (≤ 5 min) as its own retriable step.
