## 2. What the APIs allow (public docs, read Oct 23 2026)
**Meta Marketing API** (host `graph.facebook.com`, needs `ads_read`; `ads_management` only for writing, depends on `pages_read_engagement` + `pages_show_list`)
- Access: "if your app is only managing your ad account, standard access to `ads_read` and `ads_management` is sufficient"; other people's accounts need Advanced Access = App Review (+ Business Verification); Business apps get Standard automatically ([authorization](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/get-started/authorization), [permissions](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/permissions)). Separate axis, the API **tier** (renamed): *Limited* (default, "not for production apps running for live advertisers") vs *Full* (App Review; keep with 500 calls/15 days). Limited-tier hourly budget per ad account: `ads_insights` 600 + 400 × active ads, `ads_management` 300 + 40 × active ads ([rate limiting](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/overview/rate-limiting)); we need about 5 calls a day.
- Tokens: a **system-user token** (Business Settings → System users; Meta: defaults to never expiring; system user and app must belong to the same business: [system users](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/system-users/create-retrieve-update), [FB Login for Business](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/facebook-login/facebook-login-for-business)) needs no OAuth screen in our dashboard. Practitioner guide: own ad accounts work in a Development-mode app with Standard `ads_read`, no review ([Climent](https://climentmedia.com/learn/meta-marketing-api-access/)). In Live mode, Standard permissions work only for people with a role on the app.
- Insights: `GET /act_<id>/insights?level=campaign&time_increment=1&date_preset=last_28d&limit=500&fields=campaign_id,campaign_name,objective,spend,impressions,reach,clicks,inline_link_clicks,ctr,cpc,cpm,actions,cost_per_action_type,results,cost_per_result` (`date_preset`: last_7d/14d/28d/30d/90d…; `time_increment` 1–90; also adset/ad level; [reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-campaign-group/insights)). Refreshes every 15 min and settles ~28 days after the day, so re-pull the last 28 days nightly ([best practices](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/insights/best-practices)). Account currency/timezone: `GET act_<id>?fields=name,currency,timezone_name`. Throttle header `x-fb-ads-insights-throttle`; errors 4/17/613/80000-series.

**TikTok Business API** (token flow already built in `tiktokads.ts`)
- `GET https://business-api.tiktok.com/open_api/v1.3/report/integrated/get/`, header `Access-Token`; params `advertiser_id`, `report_type=BASIC`, `data_level=AUCTION_CAMPAIGN`, `dimensions=campaign_id,stat_time_day`, `metrics=spend,impressions,reach,clicks,ctr,cpc,cpm,conversion,cost_per_conversion`, `start_date`/`end_date` (YYYY-MM-DD, account timezone), `page`/`page_size`; response `data.list[{dimensions,metrics}]` + `page_info` ([OpenAPI copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/tiktok/refs/heads/main/openapi/tiktok-reporting-api-openapi.yml), [metric names](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/ingestion/lakeflow-connect/tiktok-ads-reference)). One call serves up to 20,000 ads. Names/status: `/campaign/get/`; currency/timezone: `/advertiser/info/`.
- Permissions: the developer app has a *Scope of permission* list (Ad Account Management, Ads Management, Reporting, …); TikTok reviews the app before production use (third-party: 2–7 business days) and a token carries only the scopes granted at authorization, so add **Reporting**, then reconnect once ([StackOne](https://docs.stackone.com/connectors/tiktokads/guides/connector-profile/oauth-2-0), [Adobe](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/experience-platform/sources/connectors/advertising/tiktok-ads)). No source found for a separate audit of a Direct Advertiser app reading its own advertiser.

**Google Ads API** (v26 released Oct 14 2026)
- **Developer tokens were sunset Sep 9 2026**: access levels now belong to the Cloud project and are applied for on the Google Ads API *Overview* page. Test (15,000 ops/day, test accounts only), **Explorer** (production, automated review in most cases, 2,880 ops/day), Basic (needs brand verification, automated within minutes), Standard (manual review, unlimited). **No Manager account needed** ("only if you need to link to and manage multiple accounts") ([access levels](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/api-policy/access-levels), [token page](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/api-policy/developer-token), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-drops-developer-tokens-from-ads-api-access-decisions/)).
- REST from a Worker is enough (official client libraries are gRPC): `POST https://googleads.googleapis.com/v26/customers/<10-digit id>/googleAds:search`, body `{query}`, 10,000 rows/page, `Authorization: Bearer`, scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords`, refresh at `oauth2.googleapis.com/token`; `login-customer-id` only when reaching the account through a manager ([auth](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/rest/auth), [search](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/rest/common/search)). GAQL: `SELECT campaign.id, campaign.name, campaign.status, campaign.advertising_channel_type, segments.date, metrics.cost_micros, metrics.impressions, metrics.clicks, metrics.ctr, metrics.average_cpc, metrics.conversions, metrics.cost_per_conversion, customer.currency_code FROM campaign WHERE segments.date DURING LAST_30_DAYS` (spend = `cost_micros / 1e6`; no campaign-level reach, so show impressions). `customers:listAccessibleCustomers` finds the account id after connect.

**What Metricool shows** ([Ads on Metricool](https://help.metricool.com/ads-campaigns-management-43b7j); metrics: [Meta](https://help.metricool.com/meta-ads-metrics-ahrw9), [Google](https://help.metricool.com/google-ads-metrics-2gmcs), [TikTok](https://help.metricool.com/tiktok-ads-metrics-mazta)): a top-bar **Ads** tab for Meta, Google and TikTok; table of campaigns → groups → ads (+ keywords and recommendations for Google); toolbar: search, time range, refresh, filters (status Active/Paused/Removed, platform, objective), column chooser, Create Campaign; edit budgets, pause/enable. Metrics: Meta = Impressions, Reach, Spent, Clicks, CPM, CPC, CTR, Conversion value, ROAS (+ per campaign Conversions, Results, Video views, ThruPlays, reactions); Google = Impressions, Spent, Clicks, Conversions, CPM, CPC, CTR, Conversion value, ROAS; TikTok = Impressions, Cost, Clicks, Conversions, CPM, CPC, CTR. Metricool creates Meta and Google campaigns, but **TikTok campaigns are only tracked** ([creation page](https://metricool.com/ad-campaigns-metricool/)). Useful for us: account tiles, campaign table, filters, CSV; skip ROAS (no pixel data), keywords, budget editing.

## 5. Risks
- **Reviews**: Meta none expected for his own account (verify it); TikTok Reporting scope review, time unknown, and editing the app while "TikTok accounts" is pending might restart that review; Google Explorer is automated, but a post-Sep-9 bug leaves some upgrades unhonoured (`AUTHORIZATION_ERROR`) and one outlet reports Explorer refusals for new projects in mid-October → fallback: a new Cloud project, or brand verification + Basic. The sensitive `adwords` scope keeps the unverified-app warning (fine for one user).
- **Tokens**: Meta system-user tokens can still be invalidated (password or role change); the TikTok token is long-term but scope-bound; a Google refresh token on a "Testing" consent screen dies after 7 days (his is "In production"). Meta error `#274 ad account not enabled for usage in Ads API` = account not assigned to the system user/app.
- **Quotas/cost**: all three APIs are free. Worker: about 9 KV writes/day (of 1,000), 8–12 subrequests per tick (of 50); Google 2,880 ops/day vs about 3 needed.
- **Data truth**: each platform reports in the ad account's timezone and currency (store as given, never sum across currencies); numbers settle for up to 28 days; "results" differs per objective; Google has no campaign reach.
- **Privacy/safety**: spend is business-sensitive → Bearer-only routes, not in localStorage or logs. Reading cannot get an account banned; creation can hit ad-policy and spend-limit errors (hence paused + caps).
- **Maintenance**: Google API versions sunset yearly (v26 now); pin the Meta Graph version in one constant.
