# Scout 02 · Trends: what is really trending now in hashtags and music (Instagram + TikTok)
Oct 23 2026. Public docs only; nothing logged in, repo untouched. Facts are marked **(unverified)** where I could not read the primary page.

**Bottom line.** The ban-safe, automatable answer for TikTok is already approved and half-built: the Discovery API's `trending_list`. The Radar does not use it yet (it feeds only the category pages, US only). For music, TikTok's own API chart (`cml/trending_list`) is only the licensed Commercial Music Library, not the viral songs. Instagram has no trends API at all; hashtag search needs a business-verified app and cannot discover, only look up. So: TikTok hashtags by API, music by a labelled mix (YouTube music chart + optional kworb + a weekly manual Instagram ritual), and every row proved with real post dates.

**Instagram facts.** Meta's [overview table](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/overview) lists hashtag search as Supported under Unified Login and Facebook Login, **Not supported under Instagram Login** (what our Worker uses). [Hashtag search](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-facebook-login/hashtag-search): needs App Review for "Instagram Public Content Access" + `instagram_basic`; 30 unique hashtags per rolling 7 days. [The feature page](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/features-reference/instagram-public-content-access): "not available without business verification", use cases framed around brands/campaigns. `top_media` returns caption, like/comment counts, permalink, timestamp, max 50 a page, ads excluded. Unified Login needs a business portfolio and `graph.facebook.com`. Business Discovery is documented only for Facebook Login. Third-party reporting: Instagram capped posts at 5 hashtags from Dec 2025 and says they barely move reach, so hashtag value is mainly TikTok (Metricool's 2026 study: hashtag traffic up 114%) **(secondary sources)**.

**What the reference tools do.** Metricool: planner "Add trending TikTok music" (Feb 2026 update): top 100 songs, filter by country and genre, **only for a TikTok Business account** ([update](https://metricool.com/product-updates-february-2026/)), the same shape as the CML chart; Hashtag Generator (keyword to suggestions, search is Premium); [Hashtag Tracker](https://metricool.com/instagram-hashtag-tracker/) (Instagram and X, only hashtags you enter, session credits, top 100 posts, refresh every 8 h, no trending feed); weekly editorial song rundown; its Italian guide says it does not show Reel trends. Beacons: "Trend Discovery" and a paid AI "Trending Content Search" (2 searches a day free, [plans](https://home.beacons.ai/plans)); no hashtag or sound tool found.

## 5. Risks
- **Bans:** every recommended source (rows 1-8) uses either our own approved app or public pages; none uses your logins. Rejected scrapers and Apify actors break TikTok's terms; keep them out.
- **TikTok API review:** the Accounts scope may be declined for a Personal account (unproven); Discovery data-retention/display terms not found, so keep the 7-day window, the label and the 6 h/daily refresh.
- **Honesty:** CML is not the viral chart; Creative Center audio is reported down; IG audio is manual. The UI must say "licensed tracks" or "weekly, by hand", never "trending now" for those.
- **Limits:** 50 subrequests per invocation (T1 + T2 about 8 calls, on the daily slot), KV 1,000 writes a day (one write per run), Tavily 1,000 credits a month shared, YouTube search 100 a day (T5 uses the 10,000-unit bucket only).
- **Staleness:** Discovery lists are 7-day ranks, not hourly; show `seenAt` and the window.
