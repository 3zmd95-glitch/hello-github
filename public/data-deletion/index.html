Deleting your data · 3z Prod

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What we have about you

If you commented on or messaged @3z.prod and got an automatic reply, we hold very little: your username, the first 120 characters of your comment or message and the time of the reply, in a log of the latest 50 replies. If you messaged us, also where your conversation with us stands, a record of the messages we sent you (your Instagram-scoped ID and the time) and the time of the last automatic reply you got. All of it goes away by itself: the log is replaced by newer replies, the comment ID is deleted after 7 days, and the direct-message records after about a day.

Want it deleted now?

Send a direct message to @3z.prod on Instagram saying "delete my data". We delete everything about you within 30 days and reply once it is done.

For the account owner

Remove the app's access in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → 3z Prod → Remove. Disconnecting an account in the dashboard's settings deletes its access tokens.

Edit or delete applied writing-assistant text in calendar posts. Cached assistant results expire automatically after 24 hours, and request counters after 48 hours. Deleting a browser post does not immediately clear the cache. Delete any downloaded production packs or subtitle files from your device.
